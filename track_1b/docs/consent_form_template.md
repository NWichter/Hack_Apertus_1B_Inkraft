# Consent form template: reviewers and annotators

Inkraft collects no data about people. This form is for people who help review items or grade model
answers (for example native speakers of French or Italian). Their contribution is work on the dataset, not
data about them.

---

**Project:** Inkraft, a benchmark on Swiss social and health insurance law (Team Apertool, Hack Apertus 2026)
**Contact:** hello@apertool.ch or the issue tracker of the project repository

**What you are asked to do.** Read questions and answers about Swiss federal law and either (a) check that a
question is clear and its answer matches the cited legal text, or (b) grade model answers as correct,
incorrect or not attempted. Expected time: about 1–2 hours (for example 30 items at 2–3 minutes each, or 300 judge grades at about 15 seconds each).

**What we record.** Your labels and comments, a pseudonym of your choice, and the date. We do not record
your name, e-mail address or any other personal data in the dataset.

**How it is published.** Labels and comments are released with the dataset under CDLA-Permissive-2.0 and
may be copied, changed and used by anyone, including commercially. Your pseudonym appears in the
`annotator` field. You may ask to be listed by name in the acknowledgements instead; this is optional.

**Use of AI.** Draft questions were written by language models (Claude, GPT-5.5, Gemini; candidates by Nemotron) and are disclosed as
such per item. Your labels are not used to train any model by the project.

**Voluntary.** Participation is voluntary and unpaid. You can stop at any time. Until the dataset version
that contains your labels is published, you can ask us to delete them.
