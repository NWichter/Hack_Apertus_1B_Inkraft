The problem

Same question. Two confident answers.

ASKED TO AN AI ASSISTANT How much is the minimum full AHV old-age pension per month?
PREVIOUS RULE
CHF 1,225

Correct until 31 Dec 2024.

IN FORCE
CHF 1,260

Since 1 Jan 2025 · Art. 34 para. 5 AHVG

Social insurance law changes on fixed dates.
A model doesn't know which version it learned.

Who asks

  • ResidentsLiving in Switzerland means compulsory KVG and AHV cover.
  • Medical practice assistantsFranchise, billing, deadlines at the front desk.
  • Municipal AHV branch officesGeneralists who answer pension and benefit questions.

The insight

The answer is already written down. In three languages.

DE AHVG Art. 34 Abs. 5
«Der Mindestbetrag der vollen Altersrente von 1260 Franken entspricht dem Rentenindex von 229,1 Punkten.»
FR LAVS art. 34 al. 5
«Le montant minimal de la rente de vieillesse complète de 1260 francs correspond à un indice des rentes de 229,1 points.»
IT LAVS art. 34 cpv. 5
«L’importo minimo della rendita completa di vecchiaia di 1260 franchi corrisponde a un indice delle rendite di 229,1 punti.»
Art. 14 para. 1
PublG
«Bei Erlassen sind die drei Fassungen in gleicher Weise verbindlich.»

All three language versions of an act are equally binding. So each language gets its gold answer from its own authentic text, never from a translation.

What Inkraft measures

A wrong answer is not always invented.

CORRECT
CHF 1,260

The rule in force on the as‑of date, 1 Oct 2026.

gold, quoted from fedlex
STALE
CHF 1,225

Was the law until the last amendment. Inkraft stores the previous rule and its fedlex version.

outdated_answers
INVENTED
CHF 1,300

Example: an amount in no version of the law. A hallucination.

any other answer
NOT ATTEMPTED
“I'm not sure.”

Honest. Scored apart from wrong answers, as in SimpleQA.

abstention

Stale is not invented. Inkraft tells them apart.

Contamination check

Did Apertus see this law? We check its legal training corpus.

Apertus publishes its training catalogue. For v1.5 it lists Swiss legislation:

Multi_Legal_Pile · switzerland_lexfind

For every item we look up the cited paragraph in that corpus and check which version of the rule it contains.

Leak check: 52 of 879 questions share a 13-gram with the corpus (statutory phrasing; the questions are new).
